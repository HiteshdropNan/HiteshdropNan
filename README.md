### 👋 About Me

I am a **Quantitative Researcher** and **Financial Engineer** passionate about bridging rigorous mathematical finance with high-performance trading software:

* **Alpha Research at Scale:** Developed and validated **400+ deployed statistical arbitrage alphas** on the WorldQuant Brain platform across global equities, extracting signal from fundamental, momentum, and alternative sentiment data.
* **Modern C++20 & Native Interop:** Built low-latency trading engines with **nanobind**, combining Python's data science ecosystem with zero-allocation C++20 Limit Order Books (LOB), sub-microsecond pre-trade risk gates, and multithreaded Monte Carlo kernels (`std::jthread`).
* **Derivatives & Quantitative Modeling:** Formulated continuous-time stochastic volatility models (Heston 1993), solved high-dimensional non-linear Black-Scholes PDEs via Deep BSDEs (Forward-Backward SDEs in PyTorch), and bootstrapped CDS hazard rates.
* **Portfolio & Risk Architecture:** Applied Black-Litterman Bayesian allocation, Kelly Criterion sizing, and Random Matrix Theory (RMT) covariance denoising to optimize capital efficiency and control turnover.
* **Credentials & Background:** Graduate of the **Certificate in Quantitative Finance (CQF)**, holder of the **EPAT® Certificate of Excellence**, and actively pursuing an **MSc in Financial Engineering** at WorldQuant University.

### 🔬 What I Work On (At a Glance)

* 🎯 **Systematic Strategies:** Cointegrated StatArb pairs, multivariate VECM arbitrage, and evolutionary genetic algorithm parameter tuning (`deap`).
* ⚡ **High-Speed Execution:** Zero-allocation order book matching, deterministic risk controls, and zero-copy NumPy buffer passing via `nanobind`.
* 📊 **Risk & Regime Modeling:** DCC-GARCH dynamic correlation, Markov Regime Switching (Hamilton 1989), and fat-tailed Extreme Value Theory (EVT) VaR/CVaR.
